Color Vision Check

Two short tests show whether you see colors the usual way, and if not, which kind of colorblindness you have and how strong it is. Your results are sent to Archer when you finish.

Part 1: 11 circles of dots with a number hidden in each. Part 2: spot the one circle with a little color in it, for 8 different colors. Part 3: spot which way a faint colored ring opens. Parts 2 and 3 are one tap each time.

Before you start, for a fair result:
  • Turn off Night Light / Night Shift / any blue-light filter or "warm" screen mode.
  • Turn the screen brightness up, and don't wear tinted glasses.
  • Use a normal room light, not total darkness or bright sun on the screen.
  • Sit at a normal distance from the screen and don't zoom the page.

Part 1 of 3 · dot circles

What number do you see in the circle? Look at the shape the dots make, not how bright they are. Don't stare longer than about 5 seconds.

Part 2 of 3 · odd one out

Three of these circles are grey. One has a little color in it. Tap the colored one. It gets harder as you go, so guess when you're unsure.

You can also tap the circle itself.

Part 3 of 3 · find the gap

There's a colored ring in the dots, shaped like a C. Which way does its gap point? It gets fainter as you go, so guess when you're unsure.
